Economic interpretation: GARCH assumes one statistical world. INR/USD lives in two — a calm drifting state and a crisis state. In low-vol periods GARCH outperforms BiLSTM on RMSE (0.013 vs 0.017). In high-vol periods both models converge (GARCH 0.022 vs BiLSTM 0.023) — suggesting that macro and regime features in BiLSTM add value precisely when markets are stressed and pure volatility clustering is insufficient.
